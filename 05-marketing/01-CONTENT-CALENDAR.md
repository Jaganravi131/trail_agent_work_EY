# 30-Day Launch Calendar — every post written

Rules that apply to all 30: **value first, link last.** On Reddit, answer the question completely and put the tool in a comment only if someone asks or the sub's rules permit it — a self-promo post in r/nursing gets you removed and the subreddit's moderators are within their rights. Never post from multiple accounts, never upvote yourself, never reply to your own thread pretending to be a stranger. That's astroturfing, it's detectable, and it's the fastest way to lose the only asset you have (reputation).

Cadence: 3–5 pieces/week for 30 days. Every item below is ready to paste; swap bracketed bits.

## Week 2 — food, and the first review push

**D8 · LinkedIn · the "we eat backwards" post**
> Most night workers eat backwards: not hungry at 19:00, grazing all evening, then a 900-calorie meal at 04:00 because that's when the break finally came.
> Then we come home too full to sleep, and blame ourselves.
> Digestion is under circadian control. Move the big meal into hour one and you're not being virtuous — you're just not making your gut work a night shift too.
> The one number worth taking: 30 g of protein at that first meal. A sandwich and a biscuit is 14–18 g, which is exactly why you're starving at 02:00.

**D9 · Reddit · meal-prep subs, no-fridge angle**
Title: `Night shift, no microwave, no fridge access — what I keep in a bag`
Body: the UHT milk / tuna pouches / peanuts / oatcakes / bananas stack with protein numbers, plus the flask-of-hot-water point. Genuinely useful on its own. Tool link in a comment only if asked.

**D10 · X · the snack ladder as an image post** (screenshot the table; tables travel better than links)

**D11 · LinkedIn · the story post**
> Priya, ICU nurse, 19:00 start, three nights then four off. Meal-prepped properly, ate it cold at 5 a.m. because that's when the break landed, wired till noon on the drive home, slept 4 hours, felt ill at 4 p.m., cereal and two coffees before the next shift.
> We changed four things. No new gear, no extra hours, no supplement. The anchor meal moved to 19:15 hot with 30 g protein; the 5 a.m. food became a snack; two coffee doses, both early; and the room got set up at 6:45 instead of 7:30.
> Six hours twenty minutes of sleep by week two.
> Small boring changes, correctly ordered, beat heroic effort at the wrong time. That's the entire thesis.

## Rules for the whole month

- Same handle everywhere, same one-line bio, same headshot or monogram. Discoverability across platforms is free and most people skip it.
- Never buy engagement, never use fake accounts, never post in a space that requires you to hide that you made the thing.
- If a moderator removes a post, do not repost it. Read the rule, adapt, or leave it.
- 30 days of this is worth more than 300 posts of a month's effort followed by silence — but *only* if the tracker is actually filled in weekly. The measurement is what turns posting into a business.
