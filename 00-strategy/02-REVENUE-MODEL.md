## Ebook: sale volume drivers

Assumed: 70% royalty band, delivery fee ~$0.03 for a text-only file → **$2.70 net per sale**. KENP at ~$0.0045/page × ~150 counted pages → **~$0.67 per full read**. Assume 1 read per 2 sales (typical for Select-enrolled nonfiction in a searchable niche).

Blended per "engaged unit" ≈ $2.70 + 0.5 × $0.67 ≈ **$3.04**.

## Realistic combined curve — and what it means in plain words

Base case: **~$110 in month 1, ~$650/mo by month 6, ~$900–1,300/mo by month 12** if book 2 and the kit land on schedule and you publish 3–5 pieces a week without stopping. First dollar plausibly in week 2–4 (a single $0.99 promo sale or one kit sale). First *meaningful* dollar-month: month 3–4.

Anyone promising a higher month 1 is describing an audience they already had.

What this is honestly comparable to: a slow, compounding, capital-free side asset with an expected value in the low hundreds per month at 6 months, high hundreds at 12, and a fat right tail if one distribution event hits. Not a salary. The reason to build it is that the assets don't decay, the marginal cost of book 2 is ~20% of book 1, and every piece (articles, tool, list, reviews) is inherited by the next release.

## Sensitivity — what actually moves the number

| Lever | Effect | Cost to pull |
|---|---|---|
| **Reviews** (10 → 30) | 2–3× conversion at the same traffic. The single biggest lever. | Time + asking honestly in the book's back matter |
| Ranking one mid-tail term ("how to sleep during the day") | 5–40 visits/day, compounding | 6–10 weeks of articles + internal links |
| A book in an active subreddit community that values it | One spike can be 20–100 sales | Zero money, high integrity requirement — no astroturfing |
| Price $2.99 vs $3.99 | +10–20% units, −25% unit revenue → usually net worse for a 12k-word book. Keep $3.99. | — |
| Paperback for library/union/department bulk | One order of 25 = $128 net | One PDF flyer to nursing schools & union reps (free) |
| Email list 0 → 500 | Every future launch earns $150–400 in week 1 | The newsletter is free; it's the highest-leverage *future* asset |

## Ceiling and exit criteria

Ceiling on this niche alone: ~$1,500–3,000/mo across book series + kit + list, because the addressable audience, while large, has low search volume for the exact buying phrases and the shelf is finite. The point of book 1 is not its own P&L; it's the **reusable machine** — cover template, build pipeline, author account with reviews, article site, tool site, email list. Book 2 in an adjacent niche inherits all of it.

Kill/pivot criteria (decide at day 60, not day 14):
- <15 units total and <0 reviews after 60 days of consistent publishing → the *packaging* failed, not the niche. New cover + new subtitle, re-test 30 days. Do not abandon the asset stack.
- Ranking on page 2 for 3+ terms but ~0 clicks → title/thumbnail problem. Redo cover, keep everything.
- Clicks but no sales at >1% → description or price problem, or the reviews aren't convincing. Cut to $2.99 for 10 days and read what happens.
- Sales but no reads completing → the middle sags. That's a manuscript problem; tighten chapters 3–5.
