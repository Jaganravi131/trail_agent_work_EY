## Cautions (not rejections — safeguards required)

- **The Night Shift Reset** — health: Health-adjacent: must carry a 'not medical advice' line, a 'see a clinician if…' list, and no dosing. The gate enforces these; expect to lose any claim that needs a licence to make.

## Why this ordering beats a hunch

Selection is `top_n` of the items clearing `min_total`, so a weak field yields *fewer* picks, never a filler pick. Two mediocre assets cost more than one good one: they split the same audience, halve the review count each, and teach you nothing about which lever moved the number.
