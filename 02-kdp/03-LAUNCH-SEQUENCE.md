# Launch Sequence — day 0 to day 30

Amazon's algorithm rewards early velocity and sustained conversion, in that order of importance, and it forgives a low price far more readily than it forgives a low conversion rate. Everything below is free.

## Day −3 to −1 — before it's live

- [ ] Cover done and **tested small**: screenshot a real results page for "shift work sleep", paste your cover in at thumbnail size, walk away, squint. Illegible title = not done.
- [ ] Manuscript fact-check pass complete (6 flagged claims in `00-strategy/03-90-DAY-ROADMAP.md`).
- [ ] Look Inside sample: verify chapter 1's five-numbers table is inside the free sample. If a shopper can't get value from the preview, they won't risk it. This single check moves conversion more than the price does.
- [ ] Author Central profile: bio, monogram/headshot, book claimed, "Read sample" enabled.
- [ ] Tool live on GitHub Pages, canonical URL updated, CTA pointing at the (soon) Amazon link.
- [ ] 10 people lined up for the day-20 review ask, by name. Not followers — people who've thanked you for the tool.

## Day 0 — publish

Publish at ~6 p.m. ET Tuesday–Thursday. Not Friday: weekend browsing is passive and Monday morning is where a nurse orders a book at 05:40.

Set **$0.99 for days 0–4**. This is a review and rank play, not revenue — at 35% royalty (below $2.99 you're out of the 70% band) that's ~$0.24/sale. Buy nothing with it. The purpose is 8–15 verified purchase reviews and a page in "also bought" with neighbours.

Announce once, on all channels, with the tool link **first** and the $0.99 link second. Value, then ask.

## Days 1–5 — velocity

- Daily post, different angle per day, from `05-marketing/01-CONTENT-CALENDAR.md`. Don't repost the same sentence.
- Reply to every comment within a few hours. Reply rate is free algorithmic fuel on every platform in 2026 and it's the behaviour most authors skip.
- **No** price rise yet. No advertising. No "review swap" groups — those get accounts banned.

## Day 6 — the step up

$0.99 → **$3.99**, in one move, same day as a LinkedIn/X post that says what happened and why ("launch price is over; this is where it stays"). Transparent price changes get *more* engagement than stealth ones, and it gives fence-sitters a reason to act today.

## Days 7–14 — reviews

Day 12: the 10 personal asks. Script in the calendar. Send individually, no mass DM, and always "only if it actually helped."

Day 14: reply to every review posted. Short, no defensiveness. A reply to a critical review that says "fair, fixing this in the next edition" is read by the next 400 shoppers and does more than the 1★ did damage.

## Days 12–13 and 26–27 — KDP Select free promos

If enrolled in Select, run 2 free days. Costs $0 in royalties, seeds also-bought placement and gets the book onto Kindle devices in the exact niche where word-of-mouth compounds. Schedule the promo 5–7 days ahead — the free-listing sites that index free Kindle books are free to submit to and that lead time is how you get listed.

On free days: expect hundreds of downloads, near-zero income, and 1–2 reviews. That's the trade. Do not misread it as demand — and never price the *next* book at $0.99 to recapture that spike.

## Day 15 — the paperback pitch nobody does

One PDF flyer, one email, to (a) nursing school programme coordinators, (b) union stewards and staff reps, (c) hospital/warehouse well-being mailings, (d) a couple of trade-association newsletters. Offer: the $12.99 paperback, or the print PDF for department libraries, or a free copy for their newsletter in exchange for 200 words.

Cost: 40 minutes. Hit rate: low, but one "yes" from a training coordinator is 25–60 paperbacks, and it's the only B2B door in this niche that's free to knock on.

## Day 20 — the first price test

If reviews ≥10 and units ≥1.5/day, hold $3.99.
If reviews ≥10 and units <0.5/day, test **$2.99 for 7 days** — same royalty band, better conversion, and it's a data point rather than a retreat.
If reviews <10, price is not your problem. Go back to the asks.

## Day 30 — decision

Fill the tracker for four consecutive weeks. Then, and only then, run `07-risks/02-iteration-playbook.md`. One change for the next 30 days.

## Standing rules

- Never buy ads to "validate" the book. Free distribution validated it, or nothing did.
- Never chase a rank. Ranks are an output of the four things above; watching them is how people quit at day 34.
- KENP page reads are ~40% of ebook income for a Select-enrolled short nonfiction title. If yours are lower, the sample and chapter 1 are the suspects, in that order — not the price.
- Every asset in this repo stays free to readers except the book and the kit. The tool, all 30 posts, and all three articles are the funnel, and a funnel you charge for is a wall.
